# Reflex Living Solutions — working tasks

Prepare a convincing client review first, then finish the agreed launch pages.
Proposed pages and features are not automatically agreed scope.

## Decisions and ownership

- Role-based access is not needed. Do not ask the client about it again or build it.
- **Tomiwa:** add the Reflex logo and replace all Meadowview copy, including page titles, descriptions, contact details, and footer content.
- **Dwayne:** imagery, visual layout, shared CSS, responsive behaviour, contact form, client review, and deployment.
- Coordinate before changing the same HTML sections. Dwayne can prepare image assets and shared styles while Tomiwa edits copy and branding.
- Use IKE as a reference for presenting housing, while keeping Reflex's own identity and wording.

## Before the client review

### Tomiwa — branding and copy

- [ ] Add the supplied Reflex logo across the existing pages.
- [ ] Replace Meadowview content with accurate Reflex copy; remove invented statistics, staff profiles, and care claims.
- [ ] Explain Living Solutions and Workforce Solutions using the client's wording.
- [ ] Flag missing facts rather than inventing contact details, property information, or claims.

### Dwayne — imagery and presentation

- [ ] Prepare a small photography shortlist: a welcoming home exterior for the hero, an interior, and a garden or shared living space.
- [ ] Prefer client property photos. Record each image's source and permission; identify stock photos as illustrative during review and do not present them as Reflex properties.
- [ ] Plan one photo-and-text section below the hero to break up the text-heavy layout. Add property cards only when genuine property details are available.
- [ ] Coordinate image placement with Tomiwa before editing shared HTML; keep logo and copy changes with him.
- [ ] Check photo crops on mobile and desktop, compress files, add appropriate alt text, and reserve image space to avoid layout jumps.
- [ ] Check header, navigation, buttons, spacing, and footer on mobile and desktop, including keyboard focus and horizontal overflow.

### Together — review with the client

- [ ] Review the homepage and one internal page on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] Prepare a private preview or screen-share; identify temporary imagery and missing facts.
- [ ] Bring the client call forward once those pages communicate the proposed direction. The whole site does not need to be finished first.
- [ ] Ask for feedback on visual direction, photography, service wording, navigation, and the main enquiry action.
- [ ] Request property photos and missing company/contact facts in one short list.
- [ ] Confirm launch pages and the enquiry recipient, then record one consolidated revision list.

## After feedback

- [ ] Apply the agreed copy, layout, and imagery revisions.
- [ ] Finish Home, About, and Contact; confirm additional requested pages before building them.
- [ ] Add What We Do and Our Homes if agreed, using approved service and property information.
- [ ] Add Partners, Work With Us, or tenant resources where requested and supported by supplied content. Do not reproduce IKE's whole sitemap by default.
- [ ] Connect the contact form to the agreed inbox/service; test real delivery, required fields, accessible errors, success, failure, and spam protection.
- [ ] Add accurate company details and a privacy notice appropriate to the actual form and services.
- [ ] Check for placeholders, broken links, accurate page titles/descriptions, heading order, image alt text, contrast, and keyboard use.
- [ ] Check image loading, mobile layout, zoom, and performance; test major browsers and real phones where available.

## Launch and handover

- [ ] Obtain client approval of final pages, copy, photos, and the contact journey.
- [ ] Deploy to agreed hosting and the client-owned domain; verify HTTPS.
- [ ] Test forms, links, images, and mobile behaviour on the live site.
- [ ] Add the production favicon, social preview, sitemap, robots file, and 404 page as appropriate to hosting.
- [ ] Confirm client access to the repository, domain, hosting, and form service.
- [ ] Provide a short update guide and agree future maintenance responsibilities.

## Keep scope small

- No role-based access.
- Do not add logins, dashboards, news, vacancies, analytics, or other features unless requested or already agreed elsewhere.
- Add cookie controls only if the services used require them.
- Retain checks for company details, image permissions, working enquiries, accessibility, and final client approval.
