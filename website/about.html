About Reflex Living Solutions

We provide properties for supported living, helping people find a home that supports their independence.

Our organisation

Who We Are

We provide the accommodation and work alongside care and support providers. Those providers deliver personal care and day-to-day support; Reflex focuses on the property.

Explore our housing offer
Our purpose

Our Mission

At Reflex Living Solutions, we believe that people who need support should have a place they can call their own. Our focus is on providing supported living properties that respect individual needs, preferences and independence.

What guides us

Our Values

  • 1

    Dignity First

    We respect each tenant as an individual, recognising that their home should reflect their needs and preferences.

  • 2

    Housing with Purpose

    We focus on accommodation that gives people a foundation for everyday life and greater independence.

  • 3

    Open Communication

    We listen to tenants, families and partner organisations so housing requirements and responsibilities are understood.

  • 4

    Safety & Accountability

    We take our responsibility as a property provider seriously and keep the distinction between housing and care clear.
